A one-page newspaper front page summing up Tuesday 24 November
2026's world news in a single image.

Four main stories: Zelensky urging four-way peace talks between
Ukraine, Russia, the US and Europe ("literally tomorrow"); the US
blacklisting Megaspeed, the Singapore firm at the centre of the
Nvidia spy scandal; Nvidia shares soaring 31% — their third-best
day on record — as Jensen Huang returns as CEO after pressure from
President Trump; and 24 dead in the candy-factory fire in Cikande,
Indonesia, with about 30 workers still missing. Ticker: China
sentencing an official to death for spying, Russian jets entering
South Korea's air-defence zone, the Pentagon rebuking Canada, the
EU proposing new sanctions on Russia, and Bangladesh's typhoid jabs
for 50 million children.

The hero illustration was generated in the builder session (no text
in it — a four-way negotiation table, a banned microchip, a soaring
chart, a factory-fire rescue); all headlines, the date
"TUESDAY, 24 NOVEMBER 2026" and captions were drawn programmatically
(Python + PIL, DejaVu fonts) so every word is crisp and legible.
`main.py` copies the shipped image to `/output/entry.png` after
checking it is a valid PNG within the size and dimension limits.
